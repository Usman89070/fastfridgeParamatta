Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Fridge Repair Parramatta ("we", "us", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us. This policy explains what we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have. It's written to be consistent with the Australian Privacy Principles under the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

Information We Collect

When you contact us, request a quote, or book a repair - by phone, email, or through the forms on this website - we may collect:

  • Your name and contact details (phone number, email address)
  • Your address or suburb, so we can schedule a technician
  • Details about the fault or service you need, including any photos or descriptions you provide
  • Records of your booking and job history with us, for warranty and service purposes

We do not knowingly collect sensitive information (such as health or financial account details) unless you volunteer it, and we don't request it as part of normal bookings.

How We Use Your Information

We use the information you provide to:

  • Respond to enquiries and provide quotes
  • Schedule and carry out repair appointments
  • Honour warranty claims and follow up on completed jobs
  • Send appointment confirmations and service-related communications
  • Improve our service and, where you've agreed, let you know about relevant offers

Cookies & Third-Party Services

This website loads a small number of third-party resources to function correctly, including Google Fonts (typefaces), a Tailwind CSS styling library, and the Lucide icon library. These services may receive standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type when your browser requests them, in line with their own privacy policies. We don't use these services to track you across other websites.

Sharing Your Information

We don't sell or rent your personal information. We may share it with trusted service providers who help us run the business (for example, a bookkeeper or scheduling tool), and only to the extent needed for them to do that work. We may also disclose information if required by law.

Data Security & Retention

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information we hold from misuse, loss, and unauthorised access. We keep booking and job records for as long as reasonably necessary for warranty, accounting, and legal purposes, then remove or de-identify them.

Your Rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, request a correction, or ask us to delete it, subject to any legal or warranty record-keeping requirements. Contact us using the details below and we'll respond as soon as reasonably possible.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.

Contact Us

For any privacy question or request, email info@fridgerepairparramatta.com.au.
