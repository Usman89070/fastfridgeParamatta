## 1. Research (before writing)
- Analyse the top 10 ranking competitors for the primary keyword in Parramatta, Sydney and Australia. Fetch the real pages. Note word count, headings, prices, FAQs, trust signals, schema and weaknesses.
- Never bypass blocked fetches. Say what couldn't be read in the Sources section.

## 2. Content quality
- Better than every competitor: more complete, more specific, with real prices, process, FAQs and local detail they lack.
- Unique and semantically optimised: cover the topic's entities, related questions and synonyms naturally. No copied or lightly reworded competitor text. No keyword stuffing.
- AI-search friendly: answer first (a short quick-answer box under the H1), clear question-style H2/H3s, short paragraphs, tables, FAQs with direct answers.
- Google and user friendly: matches search intent, scannable, one clear next step.
- E-E-A-T: named licensed technician, Lic #R38910 (confirm), ARCtick number, insurance, 1-year warranty, real photos, real reviews only. Use [placeholders] for unknowns (phone, fees, ARCtick, technician name). No invented stats, reviews or claims.
- Human-written: Australian English, plain sentences, varied rhythm. No em dashes. No AI tells ("delve", "in today's fast-paced world", "unlock", "genuinely", "honestly", "straightforward", "leverage", "seamless", "comprehensive guide"). No AI marks or notes inside the copy.
- No senior discount mentions.
- Prices must match website-context.md and /fridge-repair-cost. Flag any new price as [confirm].

## 3. SEO elements (every page)
- Meta title (60 characters or less) and meta description (155 or less) written for high CTR: lead with the keyword and the main benefit, add a trust or speed signal, end with a nudge. Honest, no clickbait. Provide 2 alternatives and say which is the pick. Count the characters properly.
- One H1 with the primary keyword. URL, breadcrumb, image alt text, canonical.
- Schema markup (JSON-LD) in an @graph: WebPage, Service (or BlogPosting for blogs), FAQPage, BreadcrumbList. Provider @id https://fridgerepairparramatta.com.au/#business. FAQ schema text must match the page exactly. No aggregateRating without visible, verifiable reviews. Deliver as a separate .json file and also in the document.
- Internal linking plan, written into the copy: link up to the hub, down to related spokes, across to the cost, emergency and blog pages, with natural descriptive anchor text (no "click here"). Include a table of links (anchor, target, where). Link only to pages that are live. Mark planned pages as "add when live".
- Keep the sitemap in mind: add the new URL to sitemap.xml only when it is live. Note the real live URL (the developer uses flat URLs such as /fridge-not-cooling-repair).

## 4. Design: mobile-first and responsive (mobile and desktop)
- Include a "Responsive design spec" for the page: mobile-first, single column on phones and 2 or 3 columns on tablet and desktop, sticky call and book bar on mobile, tap targets at least 44 px, readable 16 px body text, tables that scroll or stack on mobile, images in WebP with width and height set and lazy loading (hero not lazy), form fields large with the right keyboard type, no horizontal scroll, Core Web Vitals targets (LCP under 2.5 s, CLS under 0.1).
- Use the site's existing design components from website-context.md (dark slate hero, white and slate-50 sections, cards, numbered steps, price table, FAQ accordion, dark CTA band).
